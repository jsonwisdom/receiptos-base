# Physical Sovereignty Charter

**Artifact:** `PHYSICAL-SOVEREIGNTY-CHARTER`  
**Status:** `TERMINAL_LOCKED`  
**Classification:** Physical authorization framework for digital intelligence  
**Silent modification:** Prohibited

## Constitutional Boundary

This charter does not reject intelligence. It rejects the transfer of sovereignty to computation that has not received human consent, physical authorization, and verifiable evidence.

Computation produces proposals. It does not automatically produce authority over the physical world.

## Five Permanent Inscriptions

### 1. Proposal and Execution Separation

Training centers and models are proposal systems only. Real-world execution requires independent authorization by the affected local node and its physical control boundary.

### 2. Composite Identity

Device sovereignty is derived from multiple independent sources, including:

- a physical unclonable function or equivalent hardware entropy source;
- an owner-controlled key;
- measured firmware and boot state;
- local policy and jurisdictional constraints.

No single manufacturer, model provider, certificate authority, laboratory, government, or owner may unilaterally establish complete authority.

### 3. Trajectory State Machines

Safety controls must govern both individual action limits and the topology of action sequences.

A series of individually permitted actions must be rejected when their order, timing, accumulation, or physical interaction creates an unauthorized or destructive trajectory.

### 4. Time Locks and Lifeboat Recovery

Material changes to ownership, trust roots, execution policy, or high-risk permissions require an irreducible physical delay and visible notice.

Emergency recovery may restore ownership, migration, and control, but it may not disable minimum protections for human life, third parties, or public infrastructure.

### 5. Maintainability and Receipt Expiration

Hardware degradation must cause transparent restriction or safe degradation, never automatic expansion of machine authority.

Expired routine receipts may be irreversibly discarded after the applicable retention period, while preserving the minimum hashes, timestamps, authorization lineage, and legally required incident evidence needed to prove that a record existed.

Audit must not become permanent surveillance.

## Physical Customs Check

Before any model proposal becomes a real-world action, the responsible node must be able to establish:

1. **Identity is verifiable.**
2. **Authority is limited.**
3. **The proposed trajectory is compliant.**
4. **The request has passed the physical execution boundary.**

Failure of any check leaves the proposal without real-world authority.

## Sovereignty Test

A system lacks sovereign legitimacy when an ordinary person cannot:

- perceive material risk;
- pause a consequential action;
- safely exit the system;
- recover local control;
- migrate identity and memory;
- verify who changed the governing rules.

## First Principle

>
> But before receiving a receipt issued by the physical world,
> any formula it produces is only wind.

## Final State

**The gate is forged; the key is held.**

That key belongs to every person who retains the physical ability to say **no** to a computational result.
